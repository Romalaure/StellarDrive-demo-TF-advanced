# TF Advanced Wiki

The **TF Advanced** mod for *StellarDrive Demo* adds two star systems to explore, new resources, machines, building parts and space stations. This wiki describes each planet, what you find there and what it is good for.

In game, the **[cartography table](Machines.md#cartography-table)** shows the card of the planet you are on.

## Planets

### 442 Otrera system (start)
| Planet | Biome | Gravity | Air | Look for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [442 Otrera b](Otrera-b.md) | temperate (starting planet) | 1 g | breathable | iron, copper, glass |
| [TF Aridia](TF-Aridia.md) | desert | 0.9 g | breathable | quartz, iron, copper |
| [TF Petra](TF-Petra.md) | grey rock pitted with craters, dark asteroid ring | 0.45 g | none | titanium, meteorite, copper |
| [TF Altis](TF-Altis.md) | mountains up to 8 km, snow above 1,400 m, glacial lakes | 1.1 g | breathable | titanium, wood |
| [TF Verdance](TF-Verdance.md) | temperate, large lakes, forests | 0.95 g | breathable | amber, wood (oaks, birches, firs) |
| [TF Glacia](TF-Glacia.md) | glacial | 1 g | breathable | cryonite, titanium, asteroids |
| [TF Nexora](TF-Nexora.md) | futuristic | 0.85 g | breathable | quantum core, Nexoran alloy, quartz |
| [TF Sylvara](TF-Sylvara.md) | jungle | 1.05 g | breathable | amber, plenty of wood |
