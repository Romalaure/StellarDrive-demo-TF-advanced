# Research

The mod's parts are unlocked at the **research table**, in a branch of the mod linked to the game's techs. Each tech is paid in resources.

**Tabs** at the top of the table sort the techs: **Base game**, **TF Advanced** (the mod's techs by theme), **Other mods** (when there are any) and **All**.

| Tech | Requires | Unlocks | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metal construction | Iron Panels | structure, iron | 30 iron, 5 copper |
| Carpentry | Slopes | wood | 30 wood |
| Architectural glass | Glass Fabrication | glass | 20 glass, 5 iron |
| Ship computer | Wireless Signals | ship screens | 10 copper, 5 glass |
| Advanced propulsion | Chemical Propulsion | 4 engines | 25 iron, 10 copper |
| Advanced power grid | Powered Ship Controls | battery, circuit breaker | 20 copper, 10 iron |
| Artifact technology | Advanced power grid | quantum reactor, cryonite battery, force fields (compact, basic, rapid, reinforced), condenser, quantum gyroscope, exotic engine | 10 titanium, 5 cryonite, 1 artifact |
| Cartography | Beacons | cartography table | 10 copper, 10 glass, 2 meteorite |
| Station modules | Metal construction | modules, solar panels | 40 iron, 15 glass, 10 titanium |
| Nuclear power | Artifact technology | enrichment centrifuge, nuclear tank and reactor, nuclear thruster, heavy nuclear engine, nuclear field | 10 uranium, 15 titanium, 20 copper |
| Dark matter | Nuclear power | collector, tank, dark matter reactor, dark matter field generator, dark matter engine | 10 meteorite, 1 quantum core, 5 alloys |
| Dimensional portal | Dark matter | portal generator | 20 meteorite, 2 quantum cores, 3 exotic matter, 2 artifacts |
| Electronic warfare | Cartography | radars (short range, long range, deep space), stealth generator | 25 copper, 10 glass, 10 titanium, 5 quartz |
| Reinforced plating | Metal construction | reinforced iron and glass hull | 40 iron, 20 glass, 10 titanium |
| Ship mining | Advanced power grid | ship mining laser | 25 iron, 15 copper |

Setting `ResearchUnlocks = false` (section `[TFAdvanced]`): every part is available from the start.
