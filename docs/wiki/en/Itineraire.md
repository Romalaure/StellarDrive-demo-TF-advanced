# Suggested route

1. **[TF Verdance](TF-Verdance.md)**: wood, amber, the first village chests. Research *Carpentry* and *Metal construction*.
2. **[TF Altis](TF-Altis.md)** or **[TF Petra](TF-Petra.md)**: titanium; on Petra, the first **meteorites** (bring oxygen).
3. **[TF Glacia](TF-Glacia.md)**: cryonite → cryonite battery, condenser (endless oxygen), force field.
4. **[TF Nexora](TF-Nexora.md)**: quantum cores and alloy in the skyscrapers → quantum reactor.
5. **Hadès Probe**, with a force field: exotic matter.
6. Research *Dark matter*: a collector near Petra's black hole fills a tank quickly.
7. Research *Dimensional portal*, target **[Pyros](Pyros.md)** or **[Thalassa](Thalassa.md)** and go through: welcome to the **[Erebus galaxy](Galaxie-Erebe.md)**.
8. Loot the Erebus wrecks (dark matter, exotic matter) and build a [station](Stations-spatiales.md) in orbit around Thalassa.
