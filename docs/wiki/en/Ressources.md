# Resources

| Item | Where to find it | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Titanium ore** | bluish deposits on Petra, Altis, Glacia, Pyros | every advanced part |
| **Quartz crystal** | pink deposits on Aridia, Nexora, Thalassa, Cristallis | materials |
| **Cryonite** | glowing blue crystals on Glacia, Mycélia, Cristallis | cryonite battery, condenser, force field |
| **Fossil amber** | Verdance, Sylvara, Thalassa | materials |
| **Meteorite** | dark blocks with orange veins: Petra, Pyros (lots), Mycélia, Cristallis; wrecks | dark matter machines, portal, cartography table |
| **Ancient artifact fragment** | chests in temples, villages, halls; stations | quantum reactor, portal, research |
| **Quantum core** | chests in Nexora's skyscrapers, stations, wrecks | reactor, force field, gyroscope, exotic engine, dark matter machines, portal |
| **Nexoran alloy** | Nexora's city, stations, wrecks | reactor, force field, gyroscope, exotic engine, portal |
| **Exotic matter** | Hadès Probe, Erebus wrecks | exotic matter engine, portal |
| **Uranium ore** | greenish rocks on TF Aridia, TF Petra, Pyros | nuclear fuel (centrifuge), nuclear engines, research |
| **Nuclear fuel** (fluid) | enrichment centrifuge | nuclear reactor, nuclear engines |
| **Dark matter** (fluid) | dark matter collector; tanks in the Erebus wrecks | dark matter reactor, dark matter engine, portal |

**Dark matter** and **nuclear fuel** are fluids: they flow in pipes and are stored in tanks, like oxygen.

Deposits are mined with the laser like the game's rocks. Chests in buildings (once each) and in stations also hold iron, glass, copper, titanium, wood, ingots and keys.
