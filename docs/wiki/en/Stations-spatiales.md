# Space stations

**TF Station** tab. A module can be placed **on its own, straight in space or on the ground**: aim at nothing, it appears in front of you and becomes a new station. It can also be placed on a hull platform. Then **aim at a module's wall with the next one**: it attaches against it, **door to door**. It lines up with the aimed wall's door nearest to the crosshair, at the height of its floor, and turns so that one of its doors faces it. Aim at a wall without a door: it centres on that wall. Doors are 1.6 × 2.2 m. The solar panel mount goes on a module's roof.

## Building in and on modules

- Furniture and machines go on a module's floor.
- On a **wall** (inside or outside), a game object (screen, lamp, panel…) attaches as on a hull wall. The mod's building parts (planks, glass panes, beams…) lean against it while staying upright.
- On the **roof**, you place solar panels and other parts.

## Floors

The **stairwell** has two floors. A module placed against the upper door lines up with the landing (3 m). To build a second floor: a stairwell, then corridors and modules attached to its upper door.

## Lever control seat

The game's advanced pilot station (you sit in it, it flies and sends six signals) with two armrest consoles of three levers each, which follow the commands: moves on the left, rotations on the right. TF Station tab.

## Paint: outside and inside

With the TF mod's paint gun, a module has **two colors**. Aim at a wall from outside: you paint the outside. Aim at it from inside: you paint the inside (wall lining, furniture). With "Both sides" picked, both are painted at once. The gun's panel says "interior" when that is the case.

**Material**: the gun's panel also has a row "Material — station modules": Original, Metal, Wood, Dark metal, White, Glass. The chosen material applies to the aimed side (or both), on top of the color. With Glass, the walls (or the dome's cupola) become see-through.

## Air

A module is **pressurized** as soon as a module of the station (the ship and what is docked to it) is linked by its fluid socket (under the floor, at the back) to a line holding **oxygen**: the oxygen you breathe is taken from that line. The **life support module** fills it by itself (plug a tank into its air socket and power into its power sockets); a cryonite condenser can also feed it.

## Airlock

The base's entrance. Its lever, on a box against the back wall, right of the inner door, drives its two doors, never open together:
- **lever pulled towards you**: inner door (back) open, outer door (front) closed, the airlock is pressurized;
- **lever pushed towards the wall**: inner door closed, outer door open, the airlock has no air.

Attach its back door to the station and let its front door open onto the outside.

## Docking

The docking module has a **magnet** driven by its signal socket (signal > 0: magnet on). Two docking modules collar to collar pull together and dock, like the game's docking doors. Put several around a hub to host several ships, each fitted with its own docking module.
