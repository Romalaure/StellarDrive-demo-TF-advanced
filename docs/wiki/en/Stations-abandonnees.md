# Abandoned stations and wrecks

Created once per world, each with a **beacon** bearing its name (a target for the star map, the targeting computer and the portal). The beacons keep their French names.

**Getting in**: the entrance is an **open docking door** (the big round hatch). Bring your ship close and dock it with its own docking door, or step out and float in. No oxygen aboard: bring your supply.

**Inside**: chests and loot cases, supply crates, shelves, a console, a star map, a research table, bunks, tanks. Outside: a truss mast, solar panels, antennas and blinking beacons.

Stations from an older version of the mod are rebuilt (with their door) when the world loads; a destroyed pirate base stays a wreck.

## Inhabitants of the Hermes trading post

The merchant freighter is inhabited. Look at an inhabitant and press **T**:

- **Kade, the engineer** (in the trading hall, facing the terminal): **fills the tanks** of your nearest ship (within 300 m) with the fluid they already hold, for 10 iron; **charges the mod's batteries**, for 6 copper.
- **Doctor Lyra, the medic** (in the next module): **full care**, all your health and your suit's oxygen, for 3 copper.

The host does the work and takes the payment from your inventory.

They are service robots (metal bodies, visor and lights in their trade's color). They work at the post: they cannot be picked up nor recycled. To take one along, sign their **hiring contract** (20 stellar shards) in their window: you can then pick them up like a part and set them up in your ship, where they offer the same services. They cannot be built.
