# Pyros

*System: [Erebus galaxy](Galaxie-Erebe.md)*

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome | volcanic: black basalt, rusty slopes, lava lakes, red sky |
| Gravity | 1.2 g |
| Air | toxic |
| Flora | charred trees with glowing embers |

## Resources

- meteorite (lots)
- titanium
- uranium

## Places

- basalt forge ruins
- village
- freighters
- the Eye of Erebus black hole 2,500 km away
- Icarus wreck (in orbit)

## Use

The best meteorite source. Near the Eye of Erebus, a dark matter collector runs five times faster.

> ⚠️ Unbreathable air: oxygen required. Strong gravity.
