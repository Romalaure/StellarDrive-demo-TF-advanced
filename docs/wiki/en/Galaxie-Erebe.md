# Erebus galaxy

A second system, around the star **Erebus** (Érèbe), an orange giant about **0.18 light-years** from Otrera. It shows as a bright star in Otrera's sky. Warp is far too slow for such a distance: you get there through the **[portal](Machines.md#portal-generator)**, with an Erebus planet as the target.

Once there, light, day and night and the star map follow Erebus. The star map only shows the planets of the system you are in.

| Planet | Biome | Gravity | Air | Look for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Pyros](Pyros.md) | volcanic | 1.2 g | toxic | meteorite (lots), titanium |
| [TF Gaïa](TF-Gaia.md) | like the Earth: oceans, deserts, prairies, savannas, forests, mountains | 1 g | breathable | amber, plenty of wood |
| [Thalassa](Thalassa.md) | oceanic | 0.9 g | breathable | quartz, amber, palm wood |
| [Mycélia](Mycelia.md) | fungal | 0.8 g | toxic (spores) | meteorite, cryonite, mushroom wood |
| [Cristallis](Cristallis.md) | crystalline | 0.6 g | too thin | quartz (lots), cryonite, meteorite |
| [Bastion Écarlate](Bastion.md) | artificial (pirate fortress) | 1.3 g | none | uranium, meteorite, pirate bases |

## The Eye of Erebus

A black hole with a 40 km horizon, 2,500 km from Pyros: four times as massive as Petra's. Tidal forces below 79 km, warp stops at 2,400 km. Near it, a dark matter collector makes five times as much.

## Wrecks

Three gutted ships drift in orbit, each marked by its beacon (the beacons keep their French names):

| Wreck | Where | Loot |
|---|---|---|
| Épave du Vagabond (Wanderer wreck) | Thalassa orbit | 12 dark matter in a tank, meteorite, quantum core, alloy |
| Épave du Léviathan (Leviathan wreck) | Cristallis orbit | 20 dark matter, exotic matter, quantum cores, artifacts |
| Épave de l'Icare (Icarus wreck) | Pyros orbit, near the Eye | 30 dark matter, exotic matter, meteorite |

In Erebus, a dark matter collector makes **three times** as much as in Otrera.
