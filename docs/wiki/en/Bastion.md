# Bastion Écarlate

*System: [Erebus galaxy](Galaxie-Erebe.md)*

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome | artificial: dark armor plains, pools of red liquid, impact craters |
| Gravity | 1.3 g |
| Air | none |
| Flora | none |

The Scarlet Bastion: a whole planet covered in metal by the [pirates](Pirates.md). Fortresses everywhere: towers with red lights, domes, giant guns, bunkers, ramparts, landing strips. A debris ring surrounds it.

## Resources

- uranium
- meteorite
- the loot of the pirate bases

## Places

- fortresses, turrets and ramparts all over the surface
- **Base pirate : Forteresse Écarlate** and **Base pirate : Chantier de la Rouille**, in orbit (shown on the star map)
- debris ring

## Use

The pirates' lair. Their bases can be looted: chests full of quantum cores, exotic matter, Nexoran alloy, uranium; a destroyed base also leaves a big loot capsule.

> ⚠️ Pirate territory: within 5,000 km, attacks are **four times as frequent**. The bases fire at any ship under 3 km, launch missile salvos and fighters. No air, strong gravity.

See also: [Pirates](Pirates.md), [Resources](Ressources.md).
